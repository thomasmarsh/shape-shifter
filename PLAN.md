The game is that you're a shape shifter. The game is called "Shape Shifter". But you have to level up to shape shift into different animals. First, when you're on level 0, you can't morph into anything. But whenever you find candles that are lit, you can take the light of the candles with you. And if you get three lights from candles then will level up. You have to solve a puzzle to get the candles, but first you have to find the puzzle. When you're on level 1 you can shape-shift into a fairy. You have magical powers as a fairy and can make yourself a tiny fairy home with magic. You can also fly. And then instead of 3 candle lights, you need to collect 4 to get to the next level. The way you shape shift, there will be a bar with words and those words will be the animals or creatures can shape shift into, like a selector. Once you get up to level 10, then you unlock wings that let you fly long distance quickly and without exhaustion (fairies can only fly short distances and slowly before getting tired). You can also customize your character. Once you get to level 11 then you'll unlock all the animals and the game will be complete!

Underwater, you have sea pickles that produce light underwater, and these are the exact same as candles, just underwater.

- In the tutorial: you spawn in with a wooden sword which you can upgrade later to levels: stone, iron, diamond
- You also start with 100 pieces of bread
- If you die in the game, you respawn at nearby checkpoint 

Forms:
- Hearts:
	- Different maximum hearts depending on form
	- You have regenerate hearts by eating
	- Example: if you switch from human to fairy, you lose 7 hearts. switching back to human, you have to eat to regenerate health back up to 10
- Keyboard numbers 0-9 control which shape you shift to
- Movement controls are inspired by minecraft, but you can't look around: it is pseudo isometric 3D world, like Tunic
- Level 0: Human
	- 10 hearts
	- No powers
	- Get wooden sword
- Level 1: Fairy
	- 3 hearts
	- Fly slowly, short distances before exhaustion
	- Make a tiny home with magic: can hide from enemies this way, house disappears after done using it
	- Still have wooden sword, but can't use
- Level 2: Orangutan
	- 7 hearts
	- Can climb twenty trees without getting exhausted (after that they can't climb any more trees until they touch the ground)
	- Can use sword, but not as well
	- Sword levels up to stone sword
- Level 3: Bunny
	- 4 hearts
	- You can hop over things that you couldn't hop over before
	- Still have sword, but can't use it
- Level 4: Giant Winter Wolf
	- 12 hearts
	- You can run faster than you could as any other animal so far
	- Still have stone sword, but can't use it
- Level 5: Ant
	- 1 heart
	- You can climb in really tiny spaces that you couldn't before and unlock more candle lights
	- Level up to iron sword, but can't use it as an ant
- Level 6: Mermaid
	- 15 hearts
	- You have magical powers, but only underwater
	- You can swim really fast
	- Can use sword, but only underwater
	- Has water powers so it can shift any water and commanding it to be shot at an enemy: deals 3 hearts of damage
	- Can shoot bubble columns: deal 4 hearts damage
- Level 7: Cheetah
	- 11 hearts
	- Fastest of all of them
	- No bad guys can catch you if you run, but you can only run for 1 minute and it takes two minutes for you to catch your breath
	- Can't use sword in this form
- Level 8: Snake
	- 6 hearts
	- You can bite bad guys and venom will get into them and they will faint for an hour
	- You can also go into small holes, but not as small as an ant
	- Level sword up to diamond sword, but can't use in this form
- Level 9: Axolotl
	- 5 hearts
	- Regenerates health; this persists after shapeshifting from an axolotl back to another form
	- One heart restored after 3 seconds
	- Swims fast
	- Can't use sword in this form
- Level 10: No new form, just acquire wings

Bad guys:
- There are three types
	- Type 1: regular bad guys
		- Appear when you first start the game.
		- There are some testers as part of the tutorial
		- They appear when you're trying to get more candles along with other bad guys
		- They have 9 hearts
		- If they hit you with their fist it deals one heart of damage
	- Type 2: archers, has a bow and arrow
		- Only appear once you turn into a bunny
		- When they shoot at you, it deals 2 hearts of damage if they hit you with an arrow, they can also hit you with their fist for 1 heart damage
		- Has 8 hearts
	- Type 3: has a sword that deals 4 hearts of damage
		- They only have five hears of health
		- Only appear once you unlock cheetah
	- Boss:
		- Appears at the end of the game
		- There are two boss stages, first is on land, second is underwater
		- Underwater boss is harder
		- Land boss has 16 hearts
		- Land boss deals 5 hearts of damage but has slower reflexes
		- Underwater boss has 20 hearts
		- Underwater boss deals 5 hearts of damage
		- Underwater boss is quicker, land boss is slower
		- Both bosses only appear after level 10 is attained, and you have to beat them to win the game

Puzzles:
- You have to make music that matches the music you hear from a speaker. You tap squares which play different notes to hear them. You have to pick up and drag them into the right order to solve the puzzle. There is a Play button to see if you got it right. If you got it right, you get the candle light.

It's a sky world that you have to fly to when you're a fairy. You lock on a cloud island and when you first start out as a human, the game has a tutorial that teaches you how to play until you level up to a fairy to get to the next cloud island. The cloud islands have land and water areas on them.
